For ADHO 2023 in Graz, I collaborated with researchers from Temple University, led by Alex Wermer Colan, to build models comparing Temple U's New Wave Sci-Fi corpus with other curated corpora within the library. Full results from that portion of the work can be explored at https://sfnexus.io/analysis/.

We plan future publications from this work, contextualizing sci-fi New Wave's climate commitements within the larger sci-fi genre.
