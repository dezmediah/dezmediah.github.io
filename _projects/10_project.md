In Spring and Fall 2025, I collaborated with three other Emory PhD students to put on a cross-university, interdiscplinary workshop series around the
ethical concerns in the use of technology for research. We organized workshops around the following themes: Curating Ethical Datasets, Representing the Past, and
Predicting the Future. In the Fall, we invited Dr. Ari Schlesinger to give a keynote talk at Emory. 

For more on the series, you can see our website here: https://atldh.wordpress.com/.
You can also see slides I presented about the workshop at MLA 2026 in Toronto <a href="dezmiller.com/assets/pdf/thinkingcritically.pdf">here</a>.
